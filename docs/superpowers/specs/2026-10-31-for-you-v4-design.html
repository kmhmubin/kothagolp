Material 3 Expressive parts used

  • Carousel: M3 multi-browse carousel. Large, medium and small items with 28dp rounded masks that morph as you swipe.
  • Top bar: large, flexible app bar with an emphasized headline and a subtitle; it collapses on scroll.
  • Covers: 16dp rounded corners on covers; no badges on them.
  • Genres: Material shapes (cookie, sunny, clover, pill, arch, gem) in tonal colors.
  • Row titles: a filled tonal arrow button for "see all"; the "More like" switcher is a tonal chip with a dropdown.
  • ⋮ menu: the Expressive grouped vertical menu, with a switch and supporting text.
  • Long-press: your current menu, restyled as a bottom sheet with a connected button group (Open | ＋ Library).
  • Loading: the morphing wavy loading indicator, plus skeletons shaped like the real carousel and rows.
  • Motion: the Expressive spring motion scheme for the carousel, the sheet and pressed states.

Copy rules

  • Sentence case everywhere ("Not for me", not "Not Interested").
  • Reader words only: "sources", "novels", "genres". Never "pool", "indexed", "seeding", "providers", "budget", "tags", "match %".
  • One name per action: "Not for me" hides one novel; "Hide everything by this author" hides an author; "Hidden" is where to undo both.
  • Every error says what happened and what to do: "Couldn't reach the AI. Check your connection and try again."
  • Row titles say what's inside in 2–4 words; a subtitle appears only when the title can't carry it.
